A · Peak ring

Ring = O. Center line = I. Top bar = T. Right half of the bar + middle bar = F. Upper-right arc + right leg = R. The full-height peak = A.

B · Compact

No outer ring: the O is a small circle on the stem. Tall and narrow, reads like one letter, or a figure.

C · Peak frame

The A is the outer frame. Inside: stem (I), cap (T), small circle (O), its right half + right leg (R), cap + short bar (F).

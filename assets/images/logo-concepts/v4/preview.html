A · Twin F

An F and its mirror sharing one stem. Reads as a figure, or a dumbbell standing upright.

B · Stack

Three centered bars that narrow toward the bottom. Simplest of the set.

C · Open ring

A thick ring that opens at the top. A progress ring, and a C-less O.

D · Split

A disc split down the middle: in and out, two halves in balance.

E · Chevron peak

The Fortia A as a solid peak with a small second peak inside. Effort, then the next step.

F · Summit

A solid disc with a mountain peak cut out of its base.
